
**order**

**body**

```text
Your company's review tool keeps your history for your company. Prodlog keeps it for you.

If your company runs reviews in Lattice or 15Five, keep using it. That's where the official review lives, where your manager writes their assessment, and where the process your company chose actually happens. Both now offer AI-assisted review drafting inside the company's instance. Prodlog doesn't replace any of that.

Prodlog covers the part around it: you log as things happen, walk into every 1:1 prepared, write that review from evidence instead of memory, and keep the record when you leave.

## How to use both

- Keep the official review, your goals and your feedback in your company's tool.
- Log in Prodlog as things happen, from Slack, email, your phone or the web.
- Before each 1:1, copy the prep into your 1:1 doc.
- At review time, draft your answers in Prodlog from your entries, then paste the final version into your company's form.
- When you change jobs, your log comes with you.
```
