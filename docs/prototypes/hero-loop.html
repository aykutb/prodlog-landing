How the loop is scripted

No video files. Every frame is live HTML built from the dashboard tokens and Priya’s seed data, so it stays sharp, follows light and dark mode, and changes when the copy changes.

The first frame is a complete log, never a blank box. Reduced motion shows the finished frame and waits for Play. The loop pauses when it scrolls out of view.

0:00Resting frame. Priya’s log, one entry since her last 1:1 with Elena.
0:01A messy two line note types itself into the first line of the log.
0:05⌘ Enter. The note splits into two dated entries. One draws its outcome bar; the other asks for an outcome.
0:07The 1:1 card counts up to three and two strips drop into this week.
0:09Prep my 1:1. She writes what she needs from Elena and copies the prep for her 1:1 doc.
0:14The prep closes, the new rows fold away, and the loop starts again from a full frame.
